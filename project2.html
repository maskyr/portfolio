Project 02

Digital Heart
Simulator

I designed an interactive digital heart simulation that helps learners understand how heart rate, electrical activation, and ECG patterns change together in real time.

Role Designer & Developer
Timeline May - June 2026
Tools MATLAB
Team Individual
01

The context

Connect the heartbeat to
the data.

The cardiac signal is hard to understand when the heart's motion, electrical activity, and ECG data are displayed separately.

Biomedical engineering students and other learners often struggle to connect the ECG waveforms to the physical and electrical processes occurring inside the heart. In this project, all these factors are brought into one interactive simulation, allowing users to adjust the heart rate to see the effect on the heartbeat, electrical activation, ECG signal, frequency spectrum, and spectrogram.

02

The process

From signal to
simulation.

Understanding the physiology came before writing a single line of code.

I started by researching the relationship between ECG signals and the electrical activity of the heart, since I needed to understand the physiology before I could simulate it accurately. After this, I built a MATLAB pipeline that generates the signal, spectrum, and spectrogram all at once and tested it out by comparing output against known ECG waveform shapes.
