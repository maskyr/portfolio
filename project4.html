Project 04

Facial Emotion
Recognition

I built a facial emotion recognition system that uses deep learning to identify human expressions and help computers better understand emotional cues.

Role Machine Learning Developer
Timeline July – August 2026
Tools Python · TensorFlow/Keras · OpenCV
Team Individual
01

The context

Understanding the emotion behind
the expression.

While computers have the ability to identify faces, they often struggle to understand the emotional cues communicated through facial expressions.

This creates difficulties how naturally digital systems can respond to people in areas such as assistive technology, and education. In this project, I looked at how deep learning techniques can classify facial expressions and create a foundation for more emotionally aware technology.

02

The process

From pixels to
emotion.

Converting facial expressions into reliable real time predictions.

My research was concentrated on combining the pretrained facial expression model, processing camera data properly, and building an application that would allow emotion prediction in real time.

01 Research

I did research in facial expression recognition, pretrained models, image preprocessing, and the limitations that might affect the prediction, such as lighting and facial angles.

02 Build

Used TensorFlow/Keras pretrained model together with OpenCV to detect faces and output emotions from live camera input.

03 Test

Tested the application using various facial expressions, lighting, and camera angles to determine its effectiveness and to discover any mispredictions.
